Disposable Heavy-Lift Glider

Status: Concept / Prototyping | Focus: Cost-Efficiency & Physics

The Concept

This project explores a Y-Combinator style startup idea: a heavy-lift glider made of cheap, disposable materials (like reinforced cardboard or foam) designed for one-way supply drops.

Engineering Goals

  • High Payload Fraction: Minimizing airframe weight to maximize the amount of aid/cargo carried.
  • Aerodynamics: Designing high-lift wings that generate sufficient lift at low speeds without stalling.
  • Simplicity: Eliminating complex electronics. The glider relies on calculated release points and basic control surfaces rather than expensive propulsion systems.
