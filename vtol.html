Autonomous Tilt-Rotor VTOL UAV

Timeline: Aug 2024 - Present | Role: Lead Designer & Engineer

The Engineering Challenge

Designing a UAV that combines the vertical lift of a helicopter with the efficient forward flight of a fixed-wing aircraft requires complex mechanical integration. The primary challenge was creating a robust transition mechanism that could handle aerodynamic loads without adding excessive weight.

Technical Implementation

  • Tilt-Rotor Mechanism: Engineered a custom 2-axis tilt-rotor system capable of a full 90-degree transition.
  • Mechanical Design: Designed interference-free sub-assemblies that integrate bearings, servos, and carbon fiber structural elements tightly.
  • DFAM Optimization: Utilized shelling, offset split-planes, and tolerance analysis to ensure the airframe could be manufactured using FDM 3D printing despite thin-walled structures.

Simulation & Control

Beyond the hardware, I utilized ROS2 and Gazebo to simulate flight characteristics and validate the stability of the transition phase before physical prototyping.

Tools Used: SolidWorks/Inventor, ROS2 (Jazzy), Carbon Fiber Fabrication, FDM Printing.
