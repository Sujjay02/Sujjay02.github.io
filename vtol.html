VTOL Drone Development

Status: Ongoing | Focus: Vector Mechanics & Aerodynamics

The Challenge

Vertical Take-Off and Landing (VTOL) vehicles face a unique engineering challenge: the transition phase between vertical lift (helicopter mode) and forward flight (airplane mode). This requires precise calculations to maintain stability when the center of lift shifts.

My Approach

I am designing this drone with a focus on structural analysis and physics. Key areas of development include:

  • Thrust-to-Weight Optimization: Calculating the necessary motor output to lift the frame while accounting for the heavy battery load required for sustained flight.
  • Vector Mechanics: Analyzing the forces acting on the airframe during the transition phase to prevent stall spins.
  • Component Selection: Integrating motors, ESCs, and flight controllers that can handle high-amperage draws.
Key Concepts Used: Statics, Dynamics, Thermodynamics (Heat dissipation), CAD Design.
