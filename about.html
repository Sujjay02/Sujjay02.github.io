About Me

Engineer | Researcher | Builder

I'm a robotics engineering intern and researcher specializing in autonomous systems, UAV design, and multi-agent coordination. I'm currently interning at Kathedra designing precision gantry systems, while continuing research at SmartNet Lab (UNCC) on multi-drone coverage optimization with deep reinforcement learning.

My work focuses on bridging the gap between simulation and reality — building systems that are theoretically sound and practically deployable. From designing VTOL aircraft in CAD to programming distributed control algorithms in ROS2, I thrive at the intersection of mechanical design and intelligent software.

I'm co-authoring a paper on autonomous drone swarm coverage for the AIAA SciTech 2027 Student Paper Competition (Uncrewed and Autonomous Systems track), and lead InspireNC, a student-run nonprofit that has raised $35K and partnered with the US Air Force, MetLife, and Lenovo to bring engineering education to students across North Carolina.
