About Me

Engineer | Researcher | Builder

I'm a robotics engineer and researcher specializing in autonomous systems, UAV design, and multi-agent coordination. Currently pursuing my studies at UNC Charlotte, I work with the SmartNet Lab developing cutting-edge drone swarm technologies.

My work focuses on bridging the gap between simulation and reality — building systems that are theoretically sound and practically deployable. From designing VTOL aircraft in CAD to programming distributed control algorithms in ROS2, I thrive at the intersection of mechanical design and intelligent software.

Beyond research, I'm passionate about open-source development and believe in making advanced robotics accessible. Whether it's optimizing path-planning algorithms or building physical prototypes, I approach every project with a focus on elegant engineering and real-world impact.
