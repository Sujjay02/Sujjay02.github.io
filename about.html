About Me

Engineer | Researcher | Builder

I build drone swarms that teach themselves to cover the ground — and I run a nonprofit putting engineering into the hands of students who'd never otherwise touch it.

I'm a robotics engineering intern and researcher working at the intersection of mechanical design and intelligent software. At SmartNet Lab (UNC Charlotte) I'm co-authoring a paper on autonomous multi-drone coverage with deep reinforcement learning, headed for the AIAA SciTech 2027 Student Paper Competition. At Kathedra I design precision gantry systems — and I'm the person who caught the firmware mismatch that would've broken the whole ROS2 integration before a single part was ordered.

The thread through all of it is closing the gap between simulation and reality — building things that are theoretically sound and actually fly, print, and ship. From tilt-rotor VTOL aircraft in CAD to distributed control algorithms in ROS2 to a three-drone swarm learning coverage policies in Gazebo.

Away from the lab, I direct InspireNC, a student-run nonprofit that has raised $35,000 and partnered with the US Air Force, MetLife, and Lenovo to bring hands-on engineering education to students across North Carolina.

Journey

June 2026 – Present
Kathedra — Robotics Engineering Intern
Sized motor and controller for a rack-and-pinion gantry against a 385 N force ceiling; caught a firmware mismatch in the initial vendor selection that would've broken ROS2 integration. Designed a custom shaft bushing to fix a metric/imperial mismatch between motor and gearbox. Traced a 1.1 mm/cycle drift in an indexing mechanism to a gear pitch mismatch and reworked tooth count and pitch radius to fix it.
July 2025 – Present
SmartNet Lab — Research Assistant
Built a three-drone autonomous coverage simulation in ROS2/Gazebo using the MRS UAV System (Apptainer, Ubuntu 24.04) with a custom world of 25 ground targets. Implemented a Deep Q-Network coverage policy; diagnosed a convergence plateau by identifying the task as a static placement problem and built a grid-search optimizer that achieved 76% coverage as a benchmark. Co-authoring a paper for the AIAA SciTech 2027 Student Paper Competition (Uncrewed and Autonomous Systems track).
Jul 2023 – Present
InspireNC — Director
Led a student-run nonprofit building STEM pathways in North Carolina. Raised $35K over 6 months through grants, fundraisers, and donations. Partnered with the US Air Force, MetLife, and Lenovo to spread engineering education to children.
2022 – 2026
FRC 6908 Infuzed

Engineered a custom 2-axis tilt-rotor mechanism capable of 90-degree transition for VTOL operations. Optimized the airframe for Design for Additive Manufacturing (DFAM) — shelling, offset split-planes, and tolerance analysis for successful FDM printing.
