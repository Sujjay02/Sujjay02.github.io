Forge.

Sept 2025 | Focus: Generative AI & CAD

Forge is a browser-based CAD application that parses natural language into geometric parameters using the Google Gemini API.

Intent Recognition

Automated extraction of dimensions (e.g., "Create a 20mm cube") via LLM integration.
